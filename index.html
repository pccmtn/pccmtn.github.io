Martina Pocchiari

Assistant Professor of Marketing, Esade Business School

My research covers content marketing, digital marketing, and the economics of digitization. I study user-generated content on online platforms and social media, as well as marketing content in mass media. My work has appeared in the International Journal of Research in Marketing and has been presented at conferences and institutions worldwide.

I am a recipient of the AMS Mary Kay Dissertation Award and the EDAMBA Doctoral Thesis Award, and I frequently collaborate with firms and online platforms. Before joining Esade, I taught at NUS Business School. I hold a PhD in Empirical Quantitative Marketing from Erasmus University Rotterdam.

Research areas: digital marketing, user-generated content, social networks.
Methods: causal inference, structural causal modeling, text analysis, machine learning, network analysis.
