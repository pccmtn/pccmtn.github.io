Martina Pocchiari

Martina Pocchiari is Assistant Professor of Marketing at Esade Business School.
Her research areas are content marketing, digital marketing, and economics of digitization.

She focuses on studying user-generated content on online platforms and social media, and marketing content in mass media. Her research appeared in the International Journal of Research in Marketing and has been presented at leading conferences and institutions worldwide.
She is a past recipient of the AMS Mary Kay Dissertation Award and EDAMBA Doctoral Thesis Award.
She frequently collaborates with firms and online platforms for her research.

Before joining Esade, Martina was Assistant Professor of Marketing at NUS Business School, National University of Singapore, and earned her PhD in Empirical Quantitative Marketing from Erasmus University Rotterdam.

–
Main research areas and methods: Digital marketing, user-generated content, social networks; Causal inference, structural causal modeling, text analysis, machine learning, network analysis
